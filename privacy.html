Privacy Policy

The backup utility creates archives containing the Tomoor Odoo Production database and filestore. These archives may contain company, employee, and customer information.

The utility uploads archives to the configured Google Drive account using Google Drive access. Backups are retained for four days, then deleted. Temporary files created on the Odoo server while preparing an archive are removed after upload.

The utility does not intentionally share backup contents with other parties. Google Drive handles storage under the Google account owner's settings.

To stop access, the account owner can revoke the app's Google Drive access in their Google Account settings. For questions, contact the app owner using the support email shown on the consent screen.
